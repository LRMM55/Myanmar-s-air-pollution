Health Risks

Breathing in polluted airs can cause severe health issues that can interfere with your life greatly.

Health risks

Polluted air can cause asthma, breathing problems, lung disease, and heart problems.

Children and older people are more sensitive to dirty air because their bodies are weaker.

Air pollution can even cause deformations to child when the mother is exposed to high level of air pollution

  • Strokes
  • Heart diseases
  • Cancer
  • Chronic obstructive pulmonary
  • Pneumonia
  • Birth defects
