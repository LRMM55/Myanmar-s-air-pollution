Cars Involvement In The

Pollution Of Air

Air pollution is getting worse and worse every day in Myanmar. There are multiple contributors to the air pollution and one of most outstanding contributor is the car. These cars produced pollutions so much it even reached extreme level in health risk during summers. Let's address the issue.


What is air pollution?

Air pollution is contamination of the air with hazardous particles and gases which have harmful factors such as health risk, and contaminate resources such as water and food. Air pollution can cause lethal diseases such as cancer and respiratory issues. Newborns can even develop defects due to long exposure of pollution. 1.6 millions of newborns are born with defect due to the prolonged exposure of pollution from the mother. Air is more polluted in heavily urbanized or industrialized areas where there are high demands of daily necessities like travel and consumables.

Why do we have high level of air pollution?

It is primarily because of cars, vehicles and factories. They are the main contributors of air pollution and they always produce pollutions in large volume. Some people simply just burn trashes to dispose them easily and it can create carbon dioxides. Air pollution is not purely manmade. Sometimes especially during hot days, a wildfire breakout can happen and it can generate significant amount of pollutions. As of 2022, carbon dioxide emission totals about 36.4 billion metric tons and that's just carbon dioxides and excluding methane, particulate matter and such.
